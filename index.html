Protocol

Exploring the conflict between individual desire and social norms

Concept

When does an object become acceptable?

This project investigates how people react when an object is placed in a location that is not originally intended for storage. By introducing a cup holder and a tumbler above a public water dispenser, the intervention explores whether the presence of one object can encourage others to leave their belongings in the same space.

Site

The intervention was installed above a water dispenser in the library. Although the space is not officially designated for personal belongings, its proximity to a commonly used facility makes it a potential site for temporary occupation.

Intervention

A cup holder and a single tumbler were placed in the empty space above the dispenser. The installation acted as a subtle invitation, questioning whether one visible action could establish a new, unofficial rule for the space.

Observation

"If one object appears, does the space suddenly feel available?"

Reflection

The project highlights how social norms can emerge from observation rather than explicit instruction. A single object may transform the perception of a space, encouraging people to reinterpret what is acceptable behavior within a public environment.
